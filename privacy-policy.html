Privacy
Policy.

Last updated: June 2026
01

What We Collect

When you fill out our contact form, we collect only what you type in:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your phone number
  • Your business type
  • Your budget range
  • A description of your project

We don't use cookies, tracking pixels, or analytics tools that collect personal data. We don't collect any information passively as you browse the site.

02

How We Use It

The only reason we ask for your information is to respond to your inquiry. We'll use your name, email, and phone to get back to you about your project — nothing else.

We don't use your information for marketing emails, newsletters, or any automated follow-up system. If you hear from us, it's a real person responding to your specific request.

03

We Don't Sell Your Data

We don't sell, rent, trade, or share your personal information with any third party for marketing or commercial purposes. Full stop.

04

Third-Party Services

Our contact form is processed by Formspree (formspree.io). When you submit the form, your information is transmitted through their servers before landing in our inbox. Formspree has its own privacy policy you can review at formspree.io.

Other than Formspree, we do not use any third-party services that receive your personal data.

05

Data Retention

We keep your contact information only as long as needed to follow up on your inquiry and, if you become a client, to manage your project. If nothing comes of the inquiry, we don't hold onto it indefinitely.

06

Your Rights

You have the right to ask us what information we have about you, and you can request that we delete it at any time. Just email us and we'll take care of it promptly.

Contact: info@bqwebsolutions.com

07

Children's Privacy

Our website is not intended for anyone under the age of 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has submitted information through our form, please contact us and we'll delete it immediately.

08

Questions?

If you have any questions about this policy or how we handle your data, reach out — we're happy to explain.
